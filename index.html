About Us

Toko Fathul is a basic food shop located on Jln KH. Mbah ali mas ud pagerwojo. This shop has been around for more than 30 years, at first this shop was just a shop that sold petrol, as the income increased this shop began to develop into a basic food shop until now. This shop provides various types of light basic necessities such as sugar, milk, cigarettes, snacks, and others. You can come here to find various basic ingredients at cheap prices, so what are you waiting for?
